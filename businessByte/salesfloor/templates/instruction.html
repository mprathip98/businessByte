Instructions and Q&A

How do I use this website?
First, create an account and login. Then, navigate over to the dashboard page to explore and support local businesses. If you have a small business, register it under the "Add a Business" page.
How can I add my own business?
First, create an account and login. Then, navigate over to the "Add a Business" page. Enter the name and a few details about you business, including a picture. And, done! Check out your business in the dashboard page!
Are there any deals or discount right now?
Check out the Coupons page for any coupons or deals at the moment.
